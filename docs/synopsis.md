# Synopsis

Built from a read of `script/` (no guide used). Order of the middle stages is inferred from the script, so treat it as a sketch; `docs/re/flow.md` holds the map facts.

**Premise.** On Hachi Isle, Hachiemon's friends lose their lips (they pop off when straining, eating bamboo, roaring...). No lips means no eating and no chatting. Hachiemon, the only one who can understand lipless friends, sets out to collect lips for them. Tone: Kansai manzai comedy. He is the straight man and the fool by turns.

## Who is who
- **Hachiemon**: hero. Kansai speech, calls himself わし. Good-hearted, lazy-sounding, quick to heckle.
- **Lipless friends**: Cow, Whale, Penguins, Panda, Moles, Gator, Bear, Pegasus, Tyranno, Lion, plus an Egg who never had lips. They speak in animal noises with a plain-text caption under each, which is the joke: only he understands.
- **Muscle Hachiemon**: self-styled mightiest warrior of Hachi Isle, shouts every word. First boss.
- **Auntie (オバチャン)**: takoyaki lady from Osaka. Her takoyaki makes Hachiemon transform, the power-up mechanic. Rides home with a friend, bossing him all the way, and sets up a stand on Hachi Isle.
- **Tower Hachiemon**: robot in Osaka, talks in katakana (ALL CAPS here). Fights, then is moved by friendship and sends merchants to Hachi Isle.
- **Colorful Hachiemon, the sunglasses gang**: tough guys stealing lips everywhere and calling Hachiemon a villain. **Late reveal (m224-m236):** their own babies lost their lips, which is why they steal. Keep early lines plain tough-guy.
- **The Gunman**: gang's hired gun. Laconic, makes a beehive pun on Hachiemon's name (ハチ = bee), so the hero thinks the lame joke is the attack.
- **Pegasus-eating gentleman**: polite, creepy; wants to "become one" with Hachiemon by eating his lips, then falls in love.
- **Hunter couple and son**: giant fish who talk like newlyweds.
- **Boss lady and two kid brothers**: Hachiemon lectures her for a full day.
- **Space aliens**: Hachiemon insists they are octopuses.
- **Pops (おっちゃん)**: food stall owner. His stall turns up everywhere, even underwater and in the sky. Rumours of Lip Land start at his counter.
- **Yamata no Hachiemon (ヤマタノハチエモン)**: legendary monster of Lip Land, a pun on Yamata no Orochi. Final boss, ALL CAPS. **Late reveal (m282-m286):** lips are Lip Land's only resource and he has children to protect.

## Order
1. Opening narration (m000-m009), first signs, Muscle Hachiemon (m010-m042).
2. Auntie's takoyaki (m690-m739); Hachiemon resolves to go to Osaka.
3. Osaka and Tower Hachiemon (m043-m088, m337-m346). Wrong turn to Antarctica (m347-m389). Takes Aunties home (m742-m764).
4. Rescues across forest, underground, sea, sky, mountain, with talk scenes m297-m650, m660-m689, and fights in between (kid brothers, hunters, gentleman).
5. Rumours of Lip Land (m792-m803). Gang showdown with the Gunman (m183-m205), then the reveal (m206-m261).
6. Lip Land: park villain (m583-m603), Yamata no Hachiemon (m262-m274), his defeat and the dilemma (m275-m296).
7. Ending narration (m651-m659): Hachi Isle trades materials for lips and helps rebuild Lip Land as an amusement park.
